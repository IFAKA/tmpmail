tmpmail

Disposable email from the terminal. nvim-first navigation, live inbox polling, OTP and verification-link extraction.

$ tmpmail

tmpmail  tm9c12f0b4a@domain.example

> ● GitHub        Verify your email       738194
  ● Linear        Sign in                 [magic link]

j/k move · l open · Y address · n new · r refresh · ?

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IFAKA/tmpmail/main/install.sh | sh

Or, with Go:

go install github.com/IFAKA/tmpmail/cmd/tmpmail@latest

Design

Frequent operations stay on the home row. y smart-copies a high-confidence OTP or verification URL; ambiguous messages open a candidate picker instead of guessing.

Mail delivery is provided by Mail.tm. Disposable domains may be rejected by some websites.
